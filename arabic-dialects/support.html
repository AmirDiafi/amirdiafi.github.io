Support

Need help with Arabic Dialects? Email amirdiafi2@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the problem.

Common questions

  • Gems or purchases: Gem packs are billed by Apple. For refunds, contact Apple via reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Restoring your balance: Sign in with Apple in the App's Settings or Wallet to link your account.
  • Deleting recordings: Delete a take or a check result inside the App. Recordings stay on your device.
  • Deleting your account: Email us from the address you use with the App.
