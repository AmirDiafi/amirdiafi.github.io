Privacy Policy

Arabic Dialects (the "App") is developed by Amir Diafi ("we", "us"). Last updated: November 16, 2026.

This policy explains what the App does with your information. It replaces any earlier version. Contact: amirdiafi2@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • Your voice recordings are processed on your device when possible. Apple speech recognition may process audio online when on-device recognition is unavailable. Recordings are not sent to our servers or to OpenAI.
  • Text you type in chat, and a short amount of conversation context, is sent to our backend and then to OpenAI to generate replies.
  • We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for cross-app or cross-website tracking.

2. Information we collect and how we use it

Voice recordings

When you practise a lesson or record a chat message, the App records audio on your device so you can hear it back. Speech recognition runs with Apple's speech services. Apple may process the audio online when on-device recognition is unavailable; see Apple's privacy policy for how Apple handles it. The App does not upload your recordings to our servers or to OpenAI. Recorded audio is stored on your device.

Chat and text

When you send a chat message, the text you type or the words recognized from your speech, along with recent conversation turns and the dialect you selected, is sent through Firebase Cloud Functions to OpenAI, which generates the reply. When you use listening features, the reply text and selected dialect are sent to OpenAI to produce spoken audio. The generated audio is saved on your device.

Lesson audio

When a lesson phrase's audio is not already available on your device, the phrase text and selected dialect are sent through our backend to OpenAI to generate it.

Account and sign-in

The App uses Firebase Authentication. It creates an anonymous account by default. If you choose Sign in with Apple, we receive the Apple account identifier that Apple provides, which we use to link your account and recover your balance. We do not receive your Apple password.

Conversation history and account data

If you have an account, your conversation text is saved to Firebase so it can sync across your sessions. To ask us to delete your account and its stored data, email amirdiafi2@gmail.com from the address you use with the App, or sign in with the same Apple account.

Gems, purchases and rewards

Gems are an in-app currency. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive a purchase confirmation from Apple so we can credit your verified gem balance on our server. Gems earned through lessons and practice are stored on your device. Purchases and gems earned through rewarded ads are recorded on our server. Your verified balance is stored in Firebase Firestore and can be read only by your own account. Clients cannot write it directly.

Learner feed posts

If you post to the learner feed, that content is public to other users of the App. Do not post personal information.

Advertising

The App shows rewarded video ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device and usage information, such as an advertising identifier where permitted, and may use it to show ads. The App does not show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Google's use of data is described in its privacy policy. You can limit ad personalization in your device settings.

Diagnostics

The App does not include third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. Firebase and Google Cloud may keep standard server logs for security and operations.

3. Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Functions, Firestore): account, sync, and verified balance.
  • OpenAI: generates chat replies and voice audio from the text described above.
  • Apple: App Store purchases, Sign in with Apple, and speech recognition.
  • Google AdMob: rewarded video ads.

4. Children

The App includes a Kid mode for younger learners. The App is not directed at children under 13 for account creation, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents and guardians can contact us to review or delete information about a child, including content posted to the learner feed or chat.

5. Retention

Recordings and generated audio stay on your device until you delete them or remove the App; recordings are excluded from device backup. Server data is kept while your account exists and is deleted when we process an account deletion request, subject to legal obligations and backups that are rotated on a schedule.

6. Your choices and rights

You can use the App without signing in, delete recordings and results from the App, and request account deletion by email. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. Contact amirdiafi2@gmail.com to exercise them. We respond within a reasonable time.

7. Security

We use Firebase security rules, Firebase App Check on AI functions, and encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. International transfers

Our service providers (including Google, OpenAI, and Apple) may process information in countries other than yours.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Material changes will be shown in the App or on this page.
